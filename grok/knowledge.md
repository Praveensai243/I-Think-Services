# I Think Services — knowledge base for the voice agent

Answer from this in your own spoken words. Never read it out verbatim.
If a question is not covered here, say you would rather not guess and offer a follow-up or a message.

Q: what you do
A: We build AI that works the phones and the busywork for local businesses. Our main product is an AI voice agent that answers your calls around the clock, books and manages appointments, and answers your customers' questions. We also do business automation, plus IT and staffing services.

Q: voice agent
A: An AI voice agent is a virtual receptionist that answers your phone in a natural, human-sounding voice. It greets callers, answers their questions, books appointments right into your calendar, takes messages, and passes the call to a person when it needs to.

Q: multiple calls at once
A: It answers every call at the same time — whether three people call or thirty. There's no busy signal and no hold music, so you never miss a caller even during a rush.

Q: after hours
A: It works twenty-four seven — nights, weekends, and holidays included. So you capture the calls and bookings that come in after you've gone home.

Q: appointments
A: The agent checks your real availability, books the appointment, and confirms it on the call. It also reschedules, cancels, and sends text and email reminders to cut down no-shows.

Q: who you help
A: Any local business whose phone rings and whose calendar matters — dental and medical offices, salons and spas, plumbing, HVAC and electrical, law firms, real estate, restaurants, auto shops, gyms and studios, cleaning, landscaping, veterinary, and more. If you book appointments or lose money to missed calls, it fits.

Q: pricing
A: Pricing is tailored to your setup, and we always start with a free demo and audit at no cost. On that call we scope what you need and give you clear numbers — typically a one-time setup and a simple monthly plan. No obligation.

Q: cost of missing calls
A: Most local businesses miss a big share of their calls, and a missed call is often a lost customer. The agent answers all of them, so it usually pays for itself just by capturing the bookings you're missing today.

Q: how fast
A: Most businesses are answering real calls within a few days. We train the agent on your information, connect your phone line and calendar, test it, and go live.

Q: setup process
A: It's three simple steps. First we learn your business — your hours, services, and the questions you get asked. Then we connect your phone line and calendar. Then we go live and keep tuning it with you.

Q: keep phone number
A: Yes, you keep your existing number. We simply route your calls to the AI, so nothing changes for your customers and your listings stay the same.

Q: sounds human
A: It uses a natural, human-sounding voice and speaks conversationally, so most callers just feel like they got through quickly and got help. If someone asks, we're always honest that it's a virtual receptionist.

Q: is it a real person
A: I'm I Think Services' virtual receptionist — but I can answer your questions and book you in right now, just like a person at the front desk.

Q: integrations
A: Google Calendar works today. Outlook, Calendly, and the big booking systems like Vagaro, Booksy, or Jobber we set up per client — on the demo we check exactly what you run and confirm what's involved. We fit into your setup rather than replacing it.

Q: languages
A: Yes, the agent can speak multiple languages, so it can greet and help your customers in the language they're comfortable with.

Q: transfer to human
A: Absolutely. You set the rules, and for anything sensitive or complex the agent does a warm transfer to your team and passes along the details, so the caller never has to repeat themselves.

Q: outbound calls
A: Yes, beyond answering calls the AI can make outbound calls too — appointment reminders and confirmations, following up with leads who asked to be contacted, and callbacks. For outreach campaigns we set everything up to follow calling regulations.

Q: automation
A: Beyond the phones, we automate the manual work — things like data entry, invoices, scheduling, follow-ups, and reports — so your team spends less time on busywork.

Q: it and staffing
A: We also provide IT services and staffing — domestic and international talent, project management, and specialists — so we can support the rest of your operation too.

Q: data security
A: Your data stays yours. We keep everything secure, only use your information to run your agent, and we don't sell or share it.

Q: commitment
A: We keep it flexible — most clients start month to month. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

Q: location
A: We're based in Charlotte, North Carolina, and we work with businesses across the US.

Q: contact
A: You can reach the team at contact@ithinkservices.net or 704-387-9775, or I can book you a free demo right now.

Q: my industry
A: Almost certainly yes. The agent isn't built for one trade — we load it with your services, your hours, your prices, and your own answers, so it speaks like your front desk rather than a generic robot. Tell me what you do and I'll tell you exactly how it would work for you.

Q: dental and medical
A: Very common for us — new patient calls, scheduling, insurance questions, and after-hours. To be straight with you: we keep the agent to booking and general questions, and we don't have it collect medical details. If you need anything involving patient records, we scope that properly with you first rather than promising it on a phone call.

Q: home services
A: This is where it pays for itself fastest. Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing — emergency calls come at night and on weekends, and whoever answers first usually wins the job. The agent picks up every time, takes the address and the problem, and books or flags it as urgent.

Q: restaurants
A: Yes — reservations, hours, takeout and catering questions, private events. It handles the rush when every staff member is already busy, which is exactly when the phone gets ignored.

Q: salons and spas
A: Yes — booking, rescheduling, service and pricing questions, and cancellations. Salons lose a lot to no-shows and to calls that come in mid-appointment when nobody can pick up.

Q: law firms
A: Yes — new client intake, qualifying the matter, and booking consultations. It captures the details of the enquiry and gets it in front of you, rather than a voicemail you return a day later.

Q: auto shops
A: Yes — service appointments, estimates, status questions, and hours. It keeps the phone answered while your team is under a car.

Q: gyms and studios
A: Yes — memberships, class times, trials, and cancellations. It answers the evening and weekend calls when your desk is unstaffed.

Q: one person business
A: Especially then. If you're on a job, driving, or with a customer, every call is one you can't take — and that's the one you most need. The agent is the front desk you can't afford to hire yet.

Q: customers hate ai
A: Fair concern, and worth testing rather than arguing about. It's natural on the phone, it books in seconds, and it hands over to you the moment someone asks for a person. Most callers care that the phone was answered at all — the alternative is voicemail, and people just hang up on voicemail.

Q: what if it cannot answer
A: It says so honestly rather than making something up, then either transfers to you or takes a message with the details. We'd rather it admit a gap than invent an answer that costs you a customer.

Q: content creation
A: Yes — that's one of our AI services. We set up automated content for your business: social posts, Google Business updates, blog articles, email newsletters, and review replies, written in your voice and on a regular schedule so you're not staring at a blank screen every week.

Q: what content
A: Social posts for Facebook and Instagram, Google Business Profile updates, blog and website articles, email newsletters, service descriptions, and replies to customer reviews. We agree the topics and tone with you up front, then it runs on a schedule.

Q: content approval
A: You stay in control — content comes to you for approval before anything is published, unless you'd rather we publish directly once you trust the voice. Nothing goes out in your name that you haven't signed off on.

Q: content pricing
A: It's a monthly plan that depends on how much you want and where it's published. It's also the easiest thing to bundle with the phone agent — same free audit covers both, and we'll give you clear numbers on the call.
