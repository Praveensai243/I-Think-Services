# Promo reel — "The next name on the list"

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 50–60s · Higgsfield · voiceover + hard captions
**Goal:** one thing only — a local business owner in Charlotte calls our demo number.
**Watch state:** assume muted. Every line must land as on-screen text on its own.

---

## Before we build it — two things to decide

1. **We name business TYPES, not real companies.** Naming a real local business and
   saying it misses its calls is a public accusation about a company we want as a client.
   That is a defamation risk and it burns the prospect. Every "pinpoint" below hits a
   trade — dental, plumbing, salon, restaurant, law, auto — which is what actually makes
   an owner feel called out anyway ("that's me").
2. **The two stats in the hook need a source on screen or they get softened.** The
   figures below are the commonly cited ones for small-business call handling. Before
   this goes public we either put the source in the corner caption, or we change the hook
   to Version B, which claims nothing we can't prove.

---

## Production notes for Higgsfield

- 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep captions inside the middle 80% — TikTok/Reels UI eats the bottom 15%.
- 12 clips at 4–6s. Generate stills first, pick the keepers, then image-to-video — cheaper
  than re-rolling video prompts.
- Keep one visual constant across all 12 shots: **the same ringing phone motif**. It is what
  makes the turn at shot 9 read as a payoff instead of a new ad.
- One voice for the whole VO. Do not switch voices at shot 9.
- Captions: heavy sans, white on a dark plate, one line at a time, cut on the beat.
- Last frame holds the number for a full 3 seconds. That frame is the entire point of the reel.
