2. Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • What you type into the form: your name, business name, email address, phone number, the services you ticked, your preferred contact time, and your message.
  • A record of your consent: exactly which boxes you ticked, the wording you agreed to, and the date and time. We keep this because if you ever question a call from us, that record is the answer.
  • Call information: if you call us or we call you, we keep the phone number, the time and length of the call, and a transcript or recording of it, so we can honour what was agreed and improve the agent.
  • Basic site data: our host records standard request logs. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics profiling on this site.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to arrange and hold a demo, to book appointments in a calendar, to keep records of consent and of what was agreed, and to improve how our agents handle calls. Nothing else.

Who else touches it

We use a small number of service providers to run this, and they only ever process your data on our instructions: our form-delivery provider, our email provider, our website host, our calendar provider, and the voice platform that runs our AI agent. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and consent records are kept for up to three years so we can evidence consent and pick up a conversation where it stopped. Call recordings and transcripts are kept for up to twelve months. Ask us to delete yours sooner and we will, unless we are required to keep it.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent to calls or texts at any time — see section 3.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you entirely, and we will add you to our internal do-not-contact list.

Email contact@ithinkservices.net for any of these and we will action it within 30 days. Residents of states with their own privacy statutes may have additional rights, and we will honour those on request.

Children

This is a business-to-business service. The site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

3. How we call you — automated and AI voice calls

This is the section that matters most, so it is the most specific.

We only call if you ask us to

The enquiry form has a separate, unticked box asking whether we may contact you using an automated system and an artificial or prerecorded voice. Ticking it is entirely optional and it is never a condition of buying anything from us. Leave it unticked and no automated call will be placed; we will reply by email instead. There is no way to consent by accident: submitting the form alone does not do it.

What you are agreeing to if you tick it

  • We may call or text the number you gave us, using an automated dialing system and an artificial or prerecorded voice — in practice, our AI voice agent.
  • The purpose is to arrange a demo and answer questions about the services you ticked.
  • Message and data rates may apply to texts.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase.

What our agent will do on that call

  • It will tell you it is an AI voice agent and say which business it is calling from, at the start of the call.
  • It will call only between 8am and 9pm in your local time.
  • It will connect you to a person the moment you ask for one.
  • It will stop and not call again if you say so.
  • It keeps a transcript of the call, as described in section 2.

How to opt out

Any of these works, immediately and permanently: say “stop calling me” or ask for a human on a call; reply STOP to a text; or email contact@ithinkservices.net with the number to remove. We maintain an internal do-not-call list and we honour the National Do Not Call Registry.

If we got it wrong

If you received a call you did not ask for, tell us and we will find out why, remove the number, and tell you what we found. We would rather hear it from you than not.
