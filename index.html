Good questions

What businesses ask us

Will callers know it's an AI?
The agent speaks in a natural, human voice and introduces itself the way you want it to. Most callers simply feel like they got through quickly and got a helpful answer. When a call needs a person, it transfers seamlessly to your team.
Do I have to change my phone number?
No. We route your existing number to the voice agent, so nothing changes for your customers. Keep your number, keep your listings, keep your reviews.
How does it book into my calendar?
We connect directly to Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, or your scheduling system. The agent reads real availability, books the slot, and sends confirmations and reminders — all in real time, with no double-booking.
What if it can't answer something?
You decide the boundaries. For anything outside them — a sensitive issue, a VIP, a complex request — the agent does a warm transfer to the right person, passing along the full context so the caller never repeats themselves.
How long until it's live?
Most businesses are answering real calls within days. We train the agent on your info, connect your lines and calendar, test it thoroughly, and go live — then keep tuning it with you.
