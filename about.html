About us

We started I Think Services on a simple belief: most teams waste their best people on work a machine should be doing.

Why we exist

Every growing company hits the same wall — the work multiplies, so you hire, and the new people inherit the same manual grind. We think that's backwards. Before you add headcount to repetitive work, you should ask whether it needs a human at all.

I Think Services is an automation studio. We find the repetitive, error-prone, soul-draining parts of your operation and replace them with AI agents and automated workflows that run quietly in the background — reliably, around the clock, for a fraction of the cost.

How we work

We're deliberately small and senior. You talk to the people building your automation, not a layer of account managers. We scope tightly, ship in weeks not quarters, and measure success in hours saved and errors eliminated — not slides delivered.
